About

Textile engineering background, merchandising experience

I trained as a textile engineer and have spent the years since working inside the buying-house side of the garment trade — running merchandising for international clients, developing product from first sample to bulk, and managing the supply chain issues that come up along the way: costing pressure, compliance checks, quality holds, shipment timing.

That's the perspective I bring to sourcing work: I've sat on the side that answers to the brand, so I know what a factory visit report needs to say, what a costing sheet needs to hold up to, and where things usually go wrong between a tech pack and a finished carton.

I work directly with brands and small teams who want a sourcing partner on the ground in Bangladesh — not a layer of extra emails, but someone who can walk the factory floor and tell you honestly what's happening.

What I handle

Engage me for one part of the process or the whole run — from finding the right factory to signing off the finished goods.

Supplier sourcing & vetting

Shortlisting and auditing factories against your category, order volume and compliance requirements, with honest factory-floor assessments — not just a supplier list.

Merchandising & order management

Running the order calendar end to end: TNA tracking, supplier follow-up, sample approvals, and keeping production on schedule.

Product development & sampling

Turning a tech pack or a reference garment into an approved proto, fit, and pre-production sample — coordinating pattern, fabric and trim at the factory.

Costing & negotiation

Building costings that hold up — fabric, trims, CM, and freight broken out clearly — and negotiating on your behalf without sacrificing quality.

Quality assurance

In-line and pre-shipment inspection, AQL checks, and defect resolution before goods leave the factory, not after they land.

Sustainability certification guidance

Helping suppliers reach and maintain OCS, GOTS, GRS, RCS and OEKO-TEX standards, and interpreting what those certifications actually mean for your product.

How an engagement runs

A straightforward path from brief to shipped goods. Steps can be picked up individually if you already have part of this covered.

01

Brief & requirements

We go through your product, target price, volumes, and any certification or compliance requirements before anything else moves.

02

Factory shortlist & vetting

I put forward a short list of matched factories with capability, capacity and compliance status checked in person where possible.

03

Costing & negotiation

Itemised costings come back for review, with negotiation handled on your behalf until the numbers work for both sides.

04

Sampling & development

Proto through pre-production samples, with fit and construction feedback relayed clearly until you're ready to approve bulk.

05

Production & QA

In-line checks during bulk production, followed by a pre-shipment inspection against your AQL standard.

06

Shipment & handover

Documentation, shipping coordination and a final handover report, so you know exactly what shipped and how it checked out.

Certifications I work with

I help suppliers reach and document these standards, and can advise which ones actually matter for your product and market.

OCS
Organic Content Standard — tracks organic fibre from source to finished product.
GOTS
Global Organic Textile Standard — organic fibre plus environmental and labour processing criteria.
GRS
Global Recycled Standard — recycled content verification across the supply chain.
RCS
Recycled Claim Standard — chain-of-custody tracking for recycled input materials.
OEKO-TEX
Testing for harmful substances in the finished textile, batch by batch.
